Careers

A small UK team building for dog owners and the people who look after their dogs.

A small team, early on

Go Rocco is a small, early-stage team based in the UK. We are building an app that helps dog owners feel safer and better connected. If you care about dogs, privacy and products that genuinely help people, we would like to hear from you.

Why join Go Rocco

Dog-friendly everything

Bring your dog to work (even if work is your living room). We're building this for dog people.

Remote-first

Work from anywhere in the UK. We believe in flexibility and trust our team to get things done.

Early-stage impact

Shape the product from the ground up. Your ideas will directly influence what we build.

Equity options

Early team members get stock options. When Go Rocco succeeds, you succeed.

Learning budget

Annual budget for courses, conferences, and books. We invest in your growth.

Meaningful work

Build something that actually helps people and their dogs. Not ads. Not engagement hacks.

Our values

1

Dogs come first

Every decision starts with: "Does this help dogs and their owners?" If it doesn't, we don't build it.

2

Privacy by design

We collect only what we need, protect it fiercely, and give users complete control. No exceptions.

3

No judgement

Reactive dogs aren't bad dogs. Nervous owners aren't bad owners. We build for everyone.

4

Ship and learn

We'd rather launch something good today and improve it than wait for perfect. Iteration over perfection.

5

Be kind

To each other, to our users, to everyone. Life's too short for drama.
