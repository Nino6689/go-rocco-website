Dog walkers: run your business from your phone.
Free for a year, if you help us get it right.

What it is

A walker's day, done in three taps.

No spreadsheet, no WhatsApp scroll to find who's booked, no "did I invoice Sarah?". Here is a Tuesday on Go Rocco, from your phone.

  • 07:45
    Open the app. It opens on My walks.Today in order: 9:00 Biscuit & Rex (Olive), 10:30 Max (Sam), 12:00 the group. Anything a new client asked for is at the top with a Yes / No.
  • 09:00
    Tap Start.GPS starts on its own. Olive gets "Biscuit's walk has started" and can watch the route on a map, live.
  • 09:20
    Big buttons for the things that happen.Photo. Wee / poo. Fed / water. A note. One tap each. Nothing to type.
  • 10:00
    Tap Finish.The update writes itself: "Biscuit walked 2.4 km in 52 minutes, 2 toilet breaks, 3 photos". Change a word if you like, tap Send. The invoice goes with it, with a pay link.
  • Evening
    Money shows who has paid.One tap to mark cash. Your books and a tax-ready pack build themselves in the background.
🐶

Your clients get more than they get now

Each owner gets their own page: every walk, the route, photos, and their invoices, with nothing to install. If they get the free Go Rocco app they follow the walk live, get a notification when it starts and finishes, and can cancel under your own cancellation rules, not ours.

🔁

Regulars in one go

"Mon, Wed, Fri at 11" once, and four weeks land in your diary. Skip a date, pause for a holiday, or end it, from the phone. Group walks share one GPS recording and every owner still gets their own update.

🔑

The small stuff, remembered

Who holds which key, which dogs don't mix, the vet, the emergency contact, vaccination dates. Asked once.

What we need from you

Use it for real, and be blunt.

This only works if you actually run your business on it. Polite silence teaches us nothing.

  • 1
    Run your real walks on it for at least eight weeks.Start and Finish every walk from the app, so the owners get the map and the update.
  • 2
    Bring three or more of your own clients onto it.You send them their link (WhatsApp is fine). We'll help with the words. They pay nothing.
  • 3
    Thirty minutes once a month.A video call with the other founding walkers and us. What annoyed you, what your clients said, what's missing. That call sets what we build next.
  • 4
    Tell us when it's rubbish.A message in the group, or Settings → Tester programme in the app (code WALKERS) → Send feedback. Screenshots help. "This is confusing" is a complete bug report.
  • 5
    A little patience.It's early. You will find things we haven't. We fix fast, and your regulars' bookings are never at risk: everything exports in one click.

Straight answer on where we are. The app runs a walker's day today: clients, repeat walks, GPS walks, updates, invoices, books. It has been built with walkers and tested on our own dogs. Questions

The ones we get asked.

What does it cost, really?

Nothing. Founding Walkers get Pro free for 12 months. After that the core is free forever (unlimited bookings, clients, invoices, your booking page, books and the tax pack), and Pro is £15 a month if you want the extras. No card to enter now, no contract, no "we'll bill you later".

Do you take a cut of my bookings?

Not of your own clients, ever. The only fee in Go Rocco is on clients we introduce to you through the marketplace, and only for their first few bookings. You don't have to be listed on the marketplace at all.

What do my clients have to do?

Nothing they don't want to. You send them a link; it opens their own page with every walk, the route on a map, photos and their invoices. No account, no app. If they install the free Go Rocco app and sign in with the same number or email, they get the walk live on a map and a notification when you start and finish.

Does it handle group walks?

Yes. Start once with the first dog, and the other dogs on that walk share the same GPS recording. Every owner still gets their own update and their own invoice.

What happens to my data if I stop?

It's yours. Clients, bookings, invoices and books export in one click from the web portal, and we delete your account on request. We'd just ask for ten minutes on why you left.

I've got a website / another app already.

Keep them. Plenty of walkers run Go Rocco alongside something else for the first few weeks. The bit walkers switch for is the walk itself: Start, the map the owner watches, and the update that writes itself.

Android?

Yes, both. The test group starts on iPhone via TestFlight; Android testers get a Play Store link. Say which you use in your email.
