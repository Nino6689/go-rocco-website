Our story

Why we built Go Rocco, and who it is for.

It started with a walk gone wrong

You are in the park with your dog. They are off the lead and enjoying it. Then you spot another dog in the distance, and this one is on a lead.

Is that dog friendly, nervous or reactive? You squint, you wave, you try to catch the owner's eye, and you end up calling "Is he friendly?" across the park while your dog runs towards them at full speed.

Sometimes it is fine. Sometimes it really is not.

"We thought: what if you could just... know? Before you got close. Before anyone had to shout. Before things could go wrong."

That question is where Go Rocco began.

Why "Go Rocco"?

Rocco is a real dog. A beautiful, slightly chaotic mini dachshund who belongs to our co-founders, Bryony and Chez. He's friendly to a fault - the kind of dog who assumes every other dog wants to be his best friend (despite being about a tenth of their size).

But not every dog wants that. Some dogs are nervous. Some are reactive. Some are in training. And Rocco bounding over uninvited wasn't fair to them - or their owners.

We built Go Rocco so owners like us could know before we meet. So Rocco can play with dogs who want to play, and give space to dogs who don't.

What we believe

Every dog deserves understanding

Nervous dogs aren't bad dogs. Reactive dogs aren't bad dogs. They're dogs who need owners who understand them.

Community over competition

Go Rocco only works if we all use it. We're building something together, for everyone's benefit.

Privacy is not negotiable

Your location is yours. We'll never sell your data. The 50-metre rule exists because privacy matters.

Free for dog owners

Every feature in the app is free, with no adverts. The businesses that use Go Rocco pay for the extras, and only for the extras.

Made in the UK

Go Rocco is built in the UK. Our team is based here, we follow UK data protection law, and we launched here first because this is home.

We know how dogs are walked here: the parks, the pub dogs, the coastal paths, the fields that are muddier than they looked. We know it because it is our own week.

Join us

We are at the start. If you like what we are building, there are a few ways to get involved:

  • Download Go Rocco - Get the app on iOS or Android and help shape its future
  • Run your dog business on it - Go Rocco Business is free for walkers, groomers, sitters and trainers, with 0% on your own clients
  • Work with us - See the careers page

From Bryony, Chez & Rocco

Go Rocco started with a simple question from two dog owners who wanted safer walks for their mini dachshund, Rocco. What began as an idea in the park has grown into something we are proud of. Thank you for backing it. Every dog owner who joins makes walks safer for everyone. We look forward to seeing you in the park.
