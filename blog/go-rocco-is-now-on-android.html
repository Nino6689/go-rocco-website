Go Rocco Is Now on Android!

Published 6 April 2026 By the Go Rocco Team 5 min read

The wait is over. Go Rocco is officially live on the Google Play Store, and it's completely free to download.

When we launched on iOS back in March, one of the most common questions we got was "when's it coming to Android?" We heard you, and we've been working flat out to make it happen. Today, we're proud to say that every Android user in the UK can download Go Rocco and start using it right now.

Everything you love, on Android

This isn't a stripped-down version or a "lite" release. Go Rocco on Android has full feature parity with iOS. Everything that makes the app useful is here from day one:

  • GPS walk tracking: Record every walk with detailed route maps, distance, duration, and pace. Build a complete walking history for you and your dog
  • Live temperament map: Set your dog's temperament and see other dogs nearby before you cross paths. The 50-metre rule keeps everyone's privacy intact
  • Dog-friendly places: Discover pubs, cafes, parks, and beaches that welcome dogs, verified by real owners
  • Pet passport: Keep vaccination records, vet details, microchip numbers, and health notes all in one secure place
  • Social feed: Connect with other dog owners in your area. Share tips, favourite walks, and local knowledge
  • Lost pet alerts & SOS: If the worst happens, alert nearby dog owners instantly and share your dog's details with one tap
  • Achievements & rewards: Daily challenges, streaks, and leaderboards to keep your walks fun

Why Android matters

Go Rocco only works when dog owners use it together. The more people on the map, the safer walks become for everyone. With Android making up a huge share of UK smartphones, launching on Google Play was always a priority for us. It means more dogs visible on the map, more dog-friendly places reviewed, and a bigger community sharing tips and routes.

If you're an iOS user who has been telling your Android-owning friends to "just wait a bit longer" - you can finally send them the link.

Alpha Pack on Android

The Alpha Pack premium subscription is available on Android too. For those who want to go further, it's the same price and the same features:

  • Extended walk tracking (100 minutes per walk)
  • Unlimited pet profiles
  • Ask Rocco AI assistant
  • Safety zones with geofencing
  • Group chats and voice messages
  • Ad-free experience
  • Priority support

There's a 7-day free trial so you can try everything before committing. And if it's not for you, the core app is free forever.

"We built Go Rocco for every dog owner, not just the ones with iPhones. Today feels like a proper launch."

What's next

With both platforms live, our focus shifts to growing the community and shipping new features. The Apple Watch app is coming in May, and we've got a packed roadmap for the rest of 2026. Check out the full roadmap to see what's ahead.

We're also listening to every piece of feedback from our early users. If there's something you want to see in Go Rocco, tell us at info@gorocco.com. Your suggestions shape what we build next.

Download Go Rocco

Whether you're on iOS or Android, Go Rocco is free and ready to make your walks safer. Set up your dog's profile, explore the live map, and join a growing community of dog owners who believe every walk should be a good one.
