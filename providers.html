What you get, free

Your own booking page

Set your prices and hours once and get a page at book.go-rocco.com/your-name that a client can use tonight. Anyone who books there is your own client at 0%.

Finish the job and it's ready to invoice. Take card, Apple Pay and Google Pay straight into your own Stripe account, and record bank transfers and cash too.

Your policy, applied automatically. Charging for a late cancellation isn't a premium feature; it's the difference between a hobby and a business.

Repeat bookings and a waiting list

Mrs Patel every Tuesday, without writing it in the diary each week. When a slot comes free, the waiting list knows who to offer it to.

Quotes, vouchers, classes and packs

A priced proposal that only enters the diary when they say yes. Gift vouchers sold across the counter. Classes, packs and seasonal pricing.

Animal register and incident reports

Arrivals, departures and the daily record a licensing inspector asks for. A bite, an escape or a near miss written down once, properly, while it's fresh.

Keys, reviews, tips and calendar sync

How it works

About a minute to set up. Optional to be found.

1

Create your free account

Go to provider.go-rocco.com. Email and a password, no card. Or sign in with the Go Rocco app account you already have.

2

The one-minute setup

Your business name and postcode, what you offer and what it costs, then paste or upload your client list. That's the whole wizard.

3

Share your booking page

Set your hours under Availability and send clients to your page. Everything they book there is yours at 0%. Invoices go out with a pay link when the job is done.

4

Optional: get listed

To be found by Go Rocco dog owners near you, upload:

  • A selfie and photo ID
  • A basic DBS certificate under 12 months old
  • Public liability insurance, £1 million minimum
  • Your local authority licence, if you board or run a daycare

We check documents within 48 hours. Your checks then show as badges on your listing.

Fees, plainly

Only clients we introduce carry a fee, and only for a while.

Whether a client is yours or Go Rocco's introduction is decided by the system from who touched them first, never by a box you tick. When you join, the clients you already had are yours.

0%

Your own clients. Forever.

Clients you bring, import, add by hand or who find your booking page. Card payments land in your own Stripe account and Go Rocco takes nothing.

15%

A client Go Rocco introduces, on their first bookings only

For 90 days or 10 bookings, capped at £50 per client, then they're yours at 0% too. Card bookings through the app collect it automatically; bookings you invoice or take in cash are totted up on a monthly statement.

Pro and marketplace fees are separate. Your plan never changes what a client costs, and being listed never changes what the software costs.

For makers

Do you make something for dogs?

Go Rocco's shop is for independent British makers of dog things. One person at a kitchen table is as welcome as a small workshop. Your brand gets its own page, with your story and where you make things, in front of the dog owners who use Go Rocco. We're signing our founding brands now.

Apply in about ten minutes

Five short pages about what you make. It saves as you go. A person at Go Rocco reads it and decides within five working days, and we email you either way.

What you'll need

Your story and the county your things are made in, your returns window (at least 14 days, as the law requires), how long you take to post an order, and your product liability insurance certificate. Food and treats also need your feed business registration. Nothing else does.

Fair terms, paid at dispatch

You sell direct through your own Stripe account. Go Rocco's commission is 15% on item prices, never on delivery, and your payout is scheduled when you mark the order dispatched. A statement each month shows exactly what was sold and paid.

Questions

The ones providers ask us most

Is it really free?

Yes. Unlimited bookings, clients, invoices, your booking page, care records, books and the tax-ready pack, with no card and no contract. Pro is £15 a month or £144 a year and adds conveniences; it never removes anything from Free.

Do you take a cut of my own clients?

No. Anyone you bring, import, add by hand or who books through your own page is yours at 0%, forever. Only a client Go Rocco introduces carries a fee, and only on their first bookings: 15% for 90 days or 10 bookings, capped at £50 per client.

Do I have to be on the marketplace?

No. Listing is optional and your tools work either way. If you do want to be found by Go Rocco dog owners, you'll need a selfie, photo ID, a basic DBS under 12 months old and £1 million public liability insurance, plus your local authority licence if you board or run a daycare. We review within 48 hours.

Does it do my tax return?

No, and we're careful about that. It keeps tax-ready records and rolls the year up into the figures your accountant asks for, with exports for CSV, Xero, FreeAgent and QuickBooks. It's not tax advice, it doesn't file anything, and Making Tax Digital quarterly updates aren't offered yet.

Do my clients need the app?

No. Your booking page works for anyone, and every client gets a private link to their own hub with bookings, receipts, care updates and their dog's documents. Clients who use the Go Rocco app can book you from it and share their dog's full profile with you for the booking.

Can I run it from my phone?

Yes. The portal at provider.go-rocco.com works on any phone or laptop, and the Go Rocco app has a provider dashboard for your agenda, clients, earnings, keys and care logging on the go, including offline.

I already use the Go Rocco app. Do I need another account?

No. Sign in to provider.go-rocco.com with the same account and create your business from there.
